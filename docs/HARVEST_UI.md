# Harvest interface

User direction: realistic agricultural imagery, warm colors and an image-led interface.
Cream surfaces, olive navigation, terracotta actions and editorial serif headings are
shared across authentication, overview, parcel atlas, planner and platform workspaces.
Existing data workflows and capability readiness labels remain intact.

## Generated brand photograph

- Tool: built-in ImageGen, November 2026.
- Source: `public/visuals/agromax-landscape-editorial-v1.png`.
- Optimized web asset: `public/visuals/agromax-landscape-editorial-v1.webp` (1672 × 941).

  Authentication and overview include a visible caption explaining this.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a premium agricultural software's editorial brand photograph, to be used as a login page cover and a cropped dashboard feature image. Generate an exceptionally natural cinematic aerial photograph of rural Mediterranean agricultural land in western Turkey at first light: large sculpted terraced wheat fields, precise but organic olive groves, a narrow pale farm track curving through gently rolling terrain. Mature olive green, wheat gold and warm mineral soil. Side lighting, subtle morning atmosphere, real botanical textures, refined muted colors, expensive editorial landscape photography. Wide landscape framing, strong diagonal field lines, no buildings in foreground, no people or tractors. The upper third can be a quiet distant horizon; most of image is beautiful land texture. No text, labels, logos, computer interfaces, fake satellite markings, glowing borders, illustration, 3D plastic plants or excessive saturation. This is conceptual brand photography, not a measured image of the user's actual fields.
